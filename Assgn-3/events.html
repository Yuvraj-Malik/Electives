Code Clash

A competitive programming challenge where participants solve algorithmic problems against the clock.

  1. Maximum team size is 2.
  2. Participants must bring their own laptop.

  4. The team solving the most problems wins.

Battle of Bands

A musical showdown where college bands compete for the title of SEAferno's best band.

Rules

  • Maximum 6 members per band.

  • Original music is encouraged.
  • Judges' decision will be final.

Prize Pool: ₹15,000

  • Props are allowed with prior approval.

Put your knowledge to the ultimate test with questions covering technology, entertainment, sports and more.

  1. Teams consist of 2 participants.
  2. No electronic devices allowed.

  4. Highest cumulative score wins.

Prize Pool: ₹8,000

Photography Challenge

Capture the spirit of SEAferno through your lens and tell a story with a single photograph.

Treasure Hunt

Solve clues, explore the campus and race against other teams to discover the hidden treasure.

Rules

  • Teams consist of 3–5 members.

  • First team to reach the final location wins.

Prize Pool: ₹10,000
